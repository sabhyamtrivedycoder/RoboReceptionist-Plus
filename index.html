CLINICAL METHODOLOGY & ARCHITECTURE

What Does Robo Receptionist Do?

Robo Receptionist + is an autonomous optical triage workstation engineered for hospital emergency departments. It standardizes acute trauma intake through reproducible physics and deterministic clinical rules.

01 // INGESTION

Optical Trauma Ingestion

Patients presenting at late-night intake bays are scanned via 60 FPS calibrated optical sensors or high-resolution upload. The system operates fully air-gapped without relying on external cloud APIs.

02 // CONVOLUTION

3x3 Sobel & HSV Segmentation

Convolves dual-axis spatial Sobel kernels to quantify laceration edge depth while dual-hue HSV thresholding models vascular inflammation and devitalized burn tissue without observer bias.

03 // STRATIFICATION

Deterministic ESI v4 Acuity

Stratifies patient presentations into clinically validated ESI Levels 1 through 5, generating ICD-10 provisional codes and estimated hemodynamic stability indicators in sub-second time.

04 // DISPATCH

On-Call Specialist Paging

Matches patient trauma with verified on-duty surgeons, burn specialists, and ophthalmologists, instantaneously dispatching emergency pager alerts and exporting HL7/FHIR admission profiles.
