HOW IT WORKS // CLINICAL FOUNDATION

What Does Robo Receptionist Do?

Robo Receptionist + is an autonomous triage station designed for hospital trauma intake bays. It replaces subjective, slow manual intake with deterministic optical physics and standardized clinical protocols.

01 // INGESTION

Optical Trauma Ingestion

Patients presenting at late-night intake bays are scanned via 60 FPS calibrated optical sensors or high-resolution upload. The system runs air-gapped without relying on fragile internet connections.

02 // CONVOLUTION

3x3 Sobel & HSV Segmentation

Convolves dual-axis spatial Sobel kernels to quantify laceration edge depth while dual-hue HSV thresholding models vascular inflammation and devitalized burn tissue.

03 // STRATIFICATION

Deterministic ESI v4 Acuity

Stratifies patient presentations into clinically validated ESI Levels 1 through 5, generating ICD-10 provisional codes and estimated hemodynamic stability indicators.

04 // DISPATCH

On-Call Specialist Paging

Matches patient trauma with verified on-duty surgeons, burn specialists, and ophthalmologists, instantaneously dispatching emergency pager alerts and exporting HL7/FHIR admission sheets.
